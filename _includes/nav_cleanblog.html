Stroked SVG sized in `em` tracks the text and looks the same everywhere, and matches the favicon, which is stroked for the same reason. aria-hidden keeps the accessible name plain rather than having a screen reader announce the punctuation. {%- endcomment -%} {{ site.title_plain | default: site.title }}
